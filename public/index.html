Our story

About AnnaBelle

AnnaBelle is a handmade brand specialising in beautifully crafted bags made from cotton cord. Every piece is individually handmade with passion, care and attention to detail. Our designs are for those who appreciate creativity and craftsmanship.

Unlike mass-produced items, our creations are unique, individually crafted and made with pride. We believe that handmade items should be more than just beautiful – they should be special, practical and made to be loved.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about our handmade bags.

Ask a question

Are all your products handmade?

Yes! Every AnnaBelle product is carefully handmade with love, passion and attention to detail.

What materials do you use?

We use high-quality, durable polyester cord to create beautiful and practical handmade products.

Can I order a custom-made basket or bag?

Yes! We may be able to create custom designs, colours and sizes to suit your preferences. Please contact us to discuss your ideas.

How long does it take to make an order?

Production times depend on the design and availability. We will confirm the estimated delivery time when you place your order.

Do you have products ready to buy?

Yes, selected designs may be available from our current collection. Please contact us for availability.

Can I choose the colour of my product?

Absolutely! We offer a selection of colours, subject to material availability.

Are handmade products identical?

No two handmade items are exactly the same. Small variations are part of their unique charm.

How do I care for my handmade basket or bag?

We recommend gentle cleaning with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals and excessive heat.

Do you offer gift options?

Yes! Our handmade creations make lovely gifts for birthdays, special occasions and anyone who appreciates unique accessories.

Do you deliver?

Please contact us for information about available delivery options and costs.

How can I place an order?

You can contact us through our website or social media. We will be happy to help you choose your favourite design.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Returns and exchanges are subject to our store policy and applicable consumer rights. Please contact us if you have any concerns about your order.
